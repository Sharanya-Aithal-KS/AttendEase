AttendEase Professional Upgrade - Pass 3

New in this pass:
- Added a Faculty Reports & Export entry to the faculty workspace.
- Added per-section report summaries: students, subjects, classes held, present marks, absent marks, and present rate.
- Added CSV export with section, department, semester, academic year, subject, subject code, class date, student name, USN, and attendance status.
- CSV is shared using Android's Sharesheet and AndroidX FileProvider. The generated file is stored in the app cache; no storage permission is required.
- Added res/xml/file_paths.xml and the FileProvider declaration to AndroidManifest.xml.

Compatibility:
- No Room entity or schema changes in this pass.
- Existing attendance records and login workflows are retained.
- Requires AndroidX Core FileProvider, supplied transitively by AppCompat.

Testing required in Android Studio:
1. Sync Gradle and build the app.
2. Sign in as faculty and open Attendance Reports & Export.
3. Select a section and export its CSV.
4. Open the CSV in Excel or Google Sheets and verify the rows.
5. Test with an empty section and a section containing both Present and Absent records.
