AttendEase Professional Upgrade - Pass 4

This pass adds:
1. Edit/delete operations for sections, students and subjects with confirmation dialogs.
   - Section deletion cascades to students, subjects, class sessions and attendance by the existing Room foreign-key rules.
   - Student deletion removes the linked student login and attendance history.
   - Subject deletion cascades to class sessions and attendance.
2. Date-range filtered section reports and CSV export.
3. Faculty/student profile display, profile name/password update, and sign-out.
4. Light/Dark/System appearance preference persisted in SharedPreferences and applied at startup.
5. Persistent role-aware bottom navigation for Home, Sections/History, Reports and Profile.

Important:
- Existing Room schema is unchanged in this pass.
- Back up your database before testing destructive delete operations.
- Verify the app in Android Studio with Gradle Sync and an emulator/device. A full Android SDK build was not run in this environment.
- The app continues to use local Room/SQLite storage; no Firebase dependency was added.
